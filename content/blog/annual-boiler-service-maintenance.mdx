# Annual Boiler Service: What's Included and Why It Matters

A yearly boiler service is one of the best things you can do for your home. It keeps your family safe. It helps your boiler run well. It cuts your energy bills and stops big problems before they happen.

## Why Annual Boiler Servicing is Essential

### Safety First

**Carbon Monoxide Protection:**
- We test for gas leaks
- We check the flue lets fumes out safely
- We make sure the boiler burns gas properly
- We spot problems early

**Gas Safety Rules:**
- Landlords must do this by law
- Your home insurance may need it
- Your warranty may need it
- Gives you peace of mind

### Money Benefits

**Lower Energy Bills:**
- We tune your boiler to use less gas
- We adjust parts so it works at its best
- We find small problems before they get big
- Your boiler stays as good as new

**Warranty Protection:**
- Most makers need yearly servicing
- Without it, your warranty may not work
- Protects your money
- Keeps your repair cover valid

## When to Get Your Boiler Serviced

### Best Times of Year

**Good times to book:**
- **Late summer** - Before winter starts
- **One year from your last service** - Keeps warranty valid
- **After winter** - After it's worked hard all season
- **Before tenants move in** - For rental homes

### How Often by Boiler Type

**Gas Boilers:**
- Once a year is enough
- Some warranties need it twice a year
- Busy boilers may need more checks

**Oil Boilers:**
- Yearly service is a must
- A mid-winter check is a good idea
- They need more looking after

**LPG Boilers:**
- Yearly service is normal
- We check the tank and pipes too
- We test the regulator

## Problems We Often Find

### Small Problems (Easy to Stop)

**Low Pressure:**
- Why: Small leaks or bleeding radiators
- Fix: Top up pressure, fix leaks
- Stop it: Check pressure often

**Dirty Filters:**
- Why: Not cleaned, bad water
- Fix: Clean or swap the filter
- Stop it: Check filters each year

**Thermostat Problems:**
- Why: Needs resetting, flat battery
- Fix: Reset it, new battery
- Stop it: Test and change battery yearly

### Bigger Problems (We Catch Early)

**Rusty Heat Exchanger:**
- We spot this early so you don't need a new one
- We may suggest water treatment
- We may flush your system

**Burning Problems:**
- Burner may need adjusting
- Flue might be blocked
- Gas pressure might be wrong

**Safety Part Faults:**
- Pressure valve test
- Overheat switch test
- Flame sensor check

## What You Can Do Yourself
